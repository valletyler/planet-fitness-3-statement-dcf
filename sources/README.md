# Sources & Assumptions

## Forecast & Valuation Assumptions

Project assumptions are centralized on the `Inputs` sheet, including the risk-free rate, beta, equity risk premium, debt rate, long-run growth, exit multiple, and Bear / Base / Bull scenario assumptions.

The final audited build uses FY2023 actual capital expenditures as the basis for forecast capital intensity (approximately 12.7% of revenue), rather than net PP&E growth.

## Market-Context Cross-Check

The executive-summary market ranges reference a Jefferies Fitness Sector Update Q4-2023 source supplied with the project. Market multiples are used as a cross-check rather than the primary valuation method.

## Historical Price Context

The $73.00 share price is explicitly treated as the **Dec. 29, 2023 closing-price benchmark**. It is not a current market quote and the project should not be interpreted as a live investment recommendation.
